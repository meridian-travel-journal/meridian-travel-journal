    Cinque
    Terre

    Five villages, one coastline

    Five fishing villages cling to the Ligurian cliffs, linked by trails, trains and boats. Walk between them, swim off the rocks, and eat focaccia in every one.

    Base yourself in one village and use the train for the rest. Go in spring or autumn for softer light and fewer people.

    The five villages

    Monterosso al Mare

    The only proper beach, and the easiest base for families.

    Vernazza

    A tiny harbour and a castle tower. Arrive early or stay late.

    Corniglia

    The quiet one, up 382 steps. Rewarding and rarely crowded.

    Manarola

    The postcard view, best at sunset from the rocks.

    Riomaggiore

    Steep lanes, small marinas and the start of the Via dell'Amore.
