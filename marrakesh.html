    THE MERIDIAN ATLAS / Africa

    Marrakesh, Morocco

    Courtyard riads and a night-time square.

    A PLACE TO KNOW / Africa

    A city of courtyards and colour.

    Inside the medina, market lanes open onto shaded courtyards and gardens. Marrakesh is best discovered at a measured pace, with time for its food, craft and hospitality.

    01 / THE SEASON

    When to go

    Spring and autumn are often more comfortable for walking; summer afternoons can be very hot.

    02 / THE JOURNEY

    Getting there

    Marrakesh has an international airport and rail connections to other Moroccan cities. In the medina, expect to explore many lanes on foot.

    A LITTLE INSPIRATION

    While you're there.

    1. Explore the souks with time to wander
    2. Visit a garden or a historic courtyard
    3. Experience the evening food stalls around Jemaa el-Fnaa
