    Lofoten, Norway

    Where the mountains meet the sea.

    A guide to
    the islands.

    Meridian Editors
    Arctic Norway

    When to goGetting thereWhere to stayWhat to seeRate this guide

    Before the crowds find the next fjord, Lofoten still feels like the edge of something.

    When to go

    June to August brings the midnight sun. September to March gives northern lights and quiet roads.

    Getting there

    Fly to Leknes or Svolvær, or take the ferry from Bodø. A car makes the islands easy.

    Where to stay

    Book a converted fishermen's cabin (rorbu) on the water. Reserve early for summer.

    What to see

    • Reinebringen viewpoint at sunrise
    • Henningsvær harbour
    • Haukland and Uttakleiv beaches
    • The Å fishing museum
