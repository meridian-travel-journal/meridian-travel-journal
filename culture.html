    CHAPTER 01 / CULTURE & HERITAGE

    Angkor Wat, Cambodia

    Go at dawn, dress to cover shoulders and knees, and hire a local guide.

    CHAPTER 02 / CULTURE & HERITAGE

    Machu Picchu, Peru

    Book timed entry, stay on marked paths, and acclimatise in Cusco first.

    CHAPTER 03 / CULTURE & HERITAGE

    The Acropolis, Greece

    Visit late afternoon for softer light, and wear shoes with grip.

    CHAPTER 04 / CULTURE & HERITAGE

    Taj Mahal, India

    Sunrise is best. Bring little, since bag rules are strict.

    Respect checklist

    • Learn three words of the local language.
    • Ask before photographing people.
    • Cover up at religious sites.
    • Buy from local makers.
