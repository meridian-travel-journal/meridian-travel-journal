    Practical notes for a lighter, more thoughtful journey.

    OPEN A CHAPTER / 01—04

    01Packing+
    • Roll clothes and pack one layer too many.
    • Carry a day bag with a refillable bottle.
    • Leave room for what you will buy.
    02Documents+
    • Check passport validity and entry rules early.
    • Keep digital and paper copies of bookings.
    • Carry insurance details offline.
    03Money+
    • Notify your bank and carry a backup card.
    • Withdraw local cash at an ATM, not an airport kiosk.
    • Tip as locals do, not as home habits dictate.
    04Safety+
    • Share your itinerary with someone at home.
    • Keep valuables in more than one place.
    • Use licensed taxis or ride apps at night.
