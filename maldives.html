    A PLACE TO KNOW / Islands

    A slower rhythm on the water.

    Coral islands, clear lagoons and life shaped by the Indian Ocean make the Maldives a place to linger. Explore beyond the resort experience by learning about island communities and marine life.

    01 / THE SEASON

    When to go

    December to April is generally drier; the wetter months can bring showers and lower prices. Conditions vary between atolls.

    02 / THE JOURNEY

    Getting there

    International flights arrive at Malé. Depending on your island, onward travel may involve a speedboat, domestic flight or seaplane; arrange transfers with your accommodation.

    A LITTLE INSPIRATION

    While you're there.

    1. Swim or snorkel with a responsible local operator
    2. Visit an inhabited island and support local businesses
    3. Watch the changing colours of the lagoon
