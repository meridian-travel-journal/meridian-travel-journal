    A PLACE TO KNOW / Asia

    An island beyond the beach.

    Bali brings together coastal landscapes, rice fields, village life and living temple traditions. Spend time in more than one area and make space to travel respectfully.

    01 / THE SEASON

    When to go

    The drier season generally runs from April to October, though tropical showers are possible year-round.

    02 / THE JOURNEY

    Getting there

    International flights arrive near Denpasar. Road journeys can take longer than expected, so allow extra time and use a reputable local driver when needed.

    A LITTLE INSPIRATION

    While you're there.

    1. Explore the rice-field landscapes around Ubud
    2. Visit a temple respectfully in appropriate clothing
    3. Discover the coast with a locally run surf or snorkel guide
